Our Podcast: The Brief

The Brief is a Sydney University Law Society podcast dedicated to exploring non-traditional legal pathways. Every episode, we spotlight the incredible story of a guest, sharing candid conversations about careers within, outside and adjacent to the law.

Join us weekly to travel down the road less litigated. Episodes drop at 10am every Friday.
