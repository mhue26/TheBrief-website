Our Podcast: The Brief

The Brief is a Sydney University Law Society podcast dedicated to exploring non-traditional legal pathways. Every episode, we spotlight the incredible story of a guest, sharing candid conversations about careers within, outside and adjacent to the law.

Join us weekly to travel down the road less litigated. Episodes drop at 10am every Friday on Spotify.

Episode 3 · 7 Aug 2026

The Brief on Isabelle Reinecke

Isabelle Reinecke is the founder and Executive Director of Grata Fund, a leading not-for-profit driving strategic litigation to advance human rights, climate action and democratic freedoms across Australia.

Isabelle has led collaborations between leading legal experts, community advocates and civil society organisations to challenge systemic injustice, helping shape landmark cases on issues ranging from offshore refugee detention to housing for remote First Nations communities.

A Churchill Fellow and recipient of the 2022 Women’s Agenda Emerging NFP Leader Award, Isabelle is a passionate advocate for using the law as a tool for social change. Her career is a powerful example of how purpose-driven leadership, and a willingness to challenge the status quo can create lasting impact.

“Law is everywhere, and affects everything, even though it may be invisible.”
